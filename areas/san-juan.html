Getting Here

Getting to Mr. Sudz from San Juan

Pharr borders San Juan on the west, so you're heading just one city over.

  • I-2 / US 83 runs through San Juan and Pharr. Head west to reach us.

  • Coming from near the Basilica of Our Lady of San Juan del Valle? Head west into Pharr.
