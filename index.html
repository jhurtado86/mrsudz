Good to Know

Questions About Our Pharr Laundromat

What are your hours?
Mr. Sudz Laundromat is open every day of the week, 7am to 10pm. Come in early before work or late after the kids are in bed.
Where is the laundromat?
We're at 1032 W. Sam Houston Blvd., Ste. 6, in Pharr, TX 78577. It's our only location, and it's an easy drive from McAllen, San Juan, Edinburg, Alamo, and Hidalgo.
How does wash, dry & fold drop-off work?
Bring your laundry to our attendant and tell us how you like it, like your detergent, how to dry it, and any special notes. We wash, dry, and fold it, and then it's ready for you to pick up. See the wash & fold page for pricing.
Do you offer laundry pickup and delivery?
Yes. Book a pickup online and pick a time. Our driver picks up your laundry, we wash, dry, and fold it, and our driver brings it back. Pickup times for your address show up when you book.
What is there to do while my laundry runs?
Plenty. Watch the 75" big-screen TVs, hop on the free Wi-Fi, charge your phone, and grab a drink or a treat from the snack bar. The A/C stays cold, too.
Do you do ironing and dry cleaning?
Yes. We press shirts, pants, skirts, dresses, and school band uniforms, with regular or heavy starch. You can also drop off suits, dresses, coats, and sweaters for dry cleaning. For prices, give us a call or book online.
